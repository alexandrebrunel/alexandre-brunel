AI Max for Search: +60% conversions in 30 days

Account
Search, about €56k a month
My part
Recommendation, activation and report
Year
2026
  • +60%conversions in 30 days
  • +9%cost per conversion (CPA)
  • 9.4 → 7.6ROAS (return on ad spend), still on target
  • €35per additional conversion
Problem
How much more volume could Search bring this advertiser, and at what cost per conversion? The campaigns only reached the queries their keywords covered; AI Max widens matching to related searches.
What I did, the limits and the next step
Action and result
I recommended AI Max to the advertiser, switched it on across their Search campaigns, then compared the 30 days before and after and presented the results to them. Conversions went from about 2,000 to 3,200, and cost per conversion from about €28 to €31. Spend rose 73% and conversion value 41%: the extra spend returned about 5.2 times its amount.
Limits and next step
Spend rose as matching widened, so this is AI Max plus a higher spend, not AI Max alone. With no control group, seasonality is not isolated. Next: rerun it as a 50/50 campaign experiment, with a target ROAS on the extra volume.

Brand Search relaunch: from zero impressions to 72 conversions

Channel
Google Ads, Search
My part
Audit and rebuild
Before
January 2026
After
27 April – 26 May 2026 (serving from mid-May)
  • 72conversions in under two weeks of serving
  • ~€18per conversion, on about €1.3k of spend
  • 10/10Quality Score on the two main keywords (2 to 6/10 before)
Problem
The brand keywords were enabled but got no impressions: zero clicks for the whole of January. The cause was an Ad Rank failure. The ads and landing pages did not match the keywords, so the ads did not qualify for the auction, even on the brand’s own name.
What I did, the limits and the next step
Action
I added more relevant keywords, rewrote the ads and assets for each ad group, and briefed the advertiser on the landing-page content to change. The ads started serving again in mid-May.
Limits and next step
Brand searches mostly capture demand that already exists, so I report conversions and their cost rather than ROAS. Next: compare a full 30 days of serving, and track impression share on the brand terms.

Conversion tracking fixed before the move to Smart Bidding

Scope
About 50 accounts
My part
Audit and fix
Stack
Google Ads, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Consent Mode
Problem
These advertisers were about to move to Smart Bidding. Automated bidding optimises only for the conversions it can see, so every gap in tracking becomes a bidding error.
The audit, the fix and the next step
Audit and fix
Before each switch I audited the conversion setup. Enhanced conversions were disabled in Google Ads, so conversions that cookies could not attribute were not being recovered. I fixed the tracking at three levels, and about 50 accounts moved to Smart Bidding with tracking they could rely on.
Next step
Import offline conversions (qualified leads, sales) so that bidding optimises for revenue rather than form fills.
