AI Max for Search: +60% conversions in 30 days

Account
Search, about €56k a month
My part
Recommendation, activation and report
Year
2026
  • +60%conversions in 30 days
  • +9%cost per conversion (CPA)
  • 9.4 → 7.6ROAS, still on target, on spend up 73%
  • €35per additional conversion
The reasoning, step by step
Problem
How much more volume could Search bring this advertiser, and at what cost per conversion?
Diagnosis
The campaigns only reached the queries their keywords covered. AI Max widens matching to related searches: the lever to test.
Action
I recommended AI Max to the advertiser, switched it on across their Search campaigns, then compared the 30 days before and after and presented the results to them.
Result
Conversions rose 60% (about 2,000 to 3,200) with CPA within 9% of baseline (about €28 to €31). Conversion value grew 41%, slower than spend (+73%): ROAS went from 9.4 to 7.6, which still met the advertiser’s target, and the extra spend returned about 5.2.
Limits
Spend rose as matching widened, so this is AI Max plus a higher spend, not AI Max alone. With no control group, seasonality is not isolated.
Next step
Rerun it as a 50/50 campaign experiment to isolate AI Max, with a target ROAS on the extra volume.
What to look for
  1. Spend: +73% (about €56k to €97k), as the ads showed in 53% more searches.
  2. Conversions: +60% (about 2,000 to 3,200).
  3. Cost per conversion: +9% (about €28 to €31).
  4. Conversion value: +41% (no percentage on the slide). ROAS: 9.4 to 7.6.

Brand Search relaunch: from zero impressions to 72 conversions

Channel
Google Ads, Search
My part
Audit and rebuild
Before
January 2026
After
27 April – 26 May 2026 (serving from mid-May)
  • 72conversions in under two weeks of serving
  • ~€18per conversion, on about €1.3k of spend
  • 10/10Quality Score on the two main keywords (2 to 6/10 before)
The reasoning, step by step
Problem
The advertiser’s brand keywords were enabled but got no impressions at all: zero clicks for the whole of January.
Diagnosis
An Ad Rank failure. The ads and landing pages did not match the keywords: with Quality Scores of 2 to 6/10, the ads did not qualify for the auction, even on the brand’s own name.
Action
I added more relevant keywords, rewrote the ads and assets for each ad group, and briefed the advertiser on the landing-page content to change.
Result
The ads started serving again in mid-May. By 26 May, in under two weeks, the account had brought in 72 conversions at about €18 each. The account also reports conversion value, but brand searches mostly capture demand that already exists, so I report conversions and their cost rather than ROAS.
Next step
Compare a full 30 days of serving, and track impression share on the brand terms.
Before, January 2026
What to look for
  1. Clicks: flat at zero for the whole month.
  2. Impressions: 0 on every keyword.
  3. Quality Score down to 2/10, with landing page experience and expected CTR both below average.
After, 27 April – 26 May 2026
What to look for
  1. Clicks start in mid-May and reach about 300 a day.
  2. Quality Score 10/10 on the two main keywords, with all three parts above average.
  3. Top keyword: about 30 conversions at about €3 each.

Conversion tracking fixed before the move to Smart Bidding

Scope
About 50 accounts
My part
Audit and fix
Stack
Google Ads, GA4, Google Tag Manager, Consent Mode
  • ~50accounts fixed before moving to Smart Bidding
  • 3layers checked: enhanced conversions, GA4, Consent Mode
The reasoning, step by step
Problem
These advertisers were about to move to Smart Bidding. Automated bidding optimises only for the conversions it can see, so every gap in tracking becomes a bidding error.
Diagnosis
Before each switch I audited the conversion setup. The audit showed enhanced conversions disabled in Google Ads, so conversions that cookies could not attribute were not being recovered.
Action
I fixed the tracking at three levels: enhanced conversions in Google Ads, GA4 conversion data and Consent Mode signals.
Result
About 50 accounts moved to Smart Bidding with corrected conversion tracking.
Next step
Import offline conversions (qualified leads, sales) so that bidding optimises for revenue rather than form fills.
