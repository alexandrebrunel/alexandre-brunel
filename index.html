AI Max for Search: 30 days before and after

Account
Search, about €56k a month
My part
Setup and report
Year
2026

I switched on AI Max for Search across this advertiser's Search campaigns, then compared the 30 days before and after and presented the results to them.

Conversions rose 60% while cost per conversion rose 9%. The extra €41k of spend brought 1,182 more conversions at €34.90 each, or about €5.20 of conversion value for every extra euro.

What to look for
  1. Spend went from €56,159 to €97,416 (+73%), as the ads showed in 53% more searches.
  2. Conversions went from 1,982 to 3,164 (+60%).
  3. Cost per conversion went from €28.34 to €30.79 (+9%).

Brand Search account relaunch

Channel
Google Ads, Search
My part
Audit and rebuild
Before
January 2026
After
27 April – 26 May 2026

The advertiser's brand keywords were switched on but getting no impressions at all. I audited the account, then added more relevant keywords and reworked the ads, assets and landing-page content for each ad group.

The account started serving again in mid-May. By 26 May it had brought in 72 conversions at about €18 each, on €1.3k of spend.

Before, January 2026
What to look for
  1. Clicks: flat at zero for the whole month.
  2. Impressions: 0 on every keyword.
  3. Quality Score down to 2/10, with landing page and expected CTR both below average.
After, 27 April – 26 May 2026
What to look for
  1. Clicks start in mid-May and reach about 300 a day.
  2. Quality Score 10/10 on the two main keywords, with all three parts above average.
  3. Top keyword: 30.43 conversions at €3.04 each.

Responsive search ads rewritten to Excellent

Channel
Google Ads, Search
My part
Copy and assets

To improve a client's Google Ads results, I rewrote their responsive search ads, mixing Google's suggestions with my own copy: 15 distinct headlines built on the keywords people search for, new descriptions and more sitelinks. Ad Strength went up to Excellent.

Ad Strength scores how varied and relevant the assets are, not how the ad performs, so I use it as a quality check on the copy and judge the ads on conversions.

What to look for
  1. Ad strength: Excellent, the highest rating.
  2. All five of Google's suggestions are done.
  3. Headlines: 15 out of 15 used.
